About Nutri AI

Nutri AI is an advanced agricultural diagnostic tool designed to help farmers detect crop diseases early and accurately.

🚀 How it Works

  • Deep Learning: Uses an Ensemble of EfficientNet, DenseNet, and Swin Transformer.
  • Generative AI: Integrates Google Gemini to explain diseases in simple terms.
  • Offline Mode: Provides expert advice even without internet connectivity.
